# Lightfriend

## What it is
Phone-independent communication and AI service for people leaving smartphones. Lightfriend runs outside the handset and makes selected connected services available through ordinary SMS and voice calls on any phone.

## Problem it solves
People often keep a smartphone because essential contacts and services remain inside apps. Lightfriend separates selected communication and assistant functions from the handset so users can choose any phone with an ordinary SMS or call route.

## Integrations
- WhatsApp (send, receive, monitor)
- Telegram (send, receive, monitor)
- Signal (send, receive, monitor)
- Email (supported providers and IMAP-compatible accounts - fetch, send, monitor)

## Features
- Voice calling interface
- SMS chat interface
- Web search (Perplexity AI)
- Weather (7-day forecast)
- Photo analysis & translation (US, CA, AUS)
- QR code scanning (US, CA, AUS)
- Tesla vehicle control
- 24/7 critical message monitoring
- Morning/day/evening digests
- Priority sender notifications
