Questions before you switch

Do I need a phone with internet?+

No. Lightfriend works through normal voice calls and SMS. Any phone that can call and text will work.

What kinds of messages can Lightfriend surface?+

Lightfriend can surface messages from connected WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, and email accounts. Time-sensitive items can reach you by SMS, while routine updates can wait for a scheduled digest. Provider availability and AI classification can affect what arrives and when.

How does Lightfriend reduce notification noise?+

It evaluates message content together with sender relationships, timing, recent patterns, and cross-service outreach. Messages judged able to wait are collected for a digest instead of interrupting you. This is an AI judgment, so it is not guaranteed to classify every message perfectly.

Can I ask Lightfriend to send or reply to a message?+

Yes, for supported actions on connected WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, and email accounts. In the SMS and voice conversation flow, Lightfriend queues the attempt for about 60 seconds and tells you how to cancel during that window.

A queue confirmation is not proof of provider delivery. The connected service can still reject or fail the attempt.

What happens when a connected service is unavailable?+

A fetch or send can fail until the account reconnects or the provider recovers. Persistent disconnects may require reauthorizing the service; WhatsApp linked devices also require periodic activity in the native phone app.

Is Lightfriend available in my country?+

Lightfriend works with local numbers in the United States, Canada, Finland, Netherlands, United Kingdom, and Australia.

Other countries may work too. Email Rasmus to check your country before subscribing.

Can I bring my own Twilio number?+

You can connect Twilio credentials and select an eligible number already owned by that Twilio account. Lightfriend attempts to configure the required voice and SMS callbacks and provides a setup verification check.

The number still needs the right Twilio capabilities, destination permissions, and regulatory support. Setup is not guaranteed for every number or country.

Who pays Twilio charges when I bring my own number?+

Twilio bills usage for your number directly to your own Twilio account. Those provider charges are separate from the Lightfriend subscription, and higher notification, SMS, or call volume can increase them.

How much usage is included?+

Every subscription includes a $25 monthly allowance for metered Lightfriend usage, including annual subscriptions. The allowance renews monthly and does not roll over.

Actual usage depends on AI processing, destinations, carrier fees, and call duration. Once the allowance runs out, metered usage pauses unless you enable pay-as-you-go overage with a valid payment method. Bring-your-own-Twilio charges are separate and billed by Twilio.

How is Lightfriend designed with privacy in mind?+

Lightfriend is open source and documents a production architecture that uses an AWS Nitro Enclave and encrypted storage. Published build and enclave information is intended to make parts of that architecture independently inspectable.

Connected providers and phone carriers still process their parts of a communication. Optional voice calls use an external AI service outside Lightfriend’s independently verifiable environment.
