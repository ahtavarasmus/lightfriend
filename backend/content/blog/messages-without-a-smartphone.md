Leaving a smartphone often means choosing between attention and reachability. The people you care about may still use WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, or email even when you no longer want those apps in your pocket.

Lightfriend connects supported accounts to a simpler interface: ordinary SMS and voice calls. You can ask about recent messages, search for a sender or topic, read the relevant context, and reply through the connected service.

## The services remain separate

Lightfriend does not turn every inbox into one undifferentiated stream. Messages retain their service, conversation, sender, and direction. Email also retains mailbox and account identity so a query or reply can use the intended connected inbox.

That separation is important when the same person contacts you in more than one place or when you have several email accounts. It reduces the chance that a reply is sent through the wrong identity.

## Access without the feed

The goal is selective access, not a complete clone of each provider’s app. Rich media, provider-specific features, and live delivery behavior may vary. A disconnected or unavailable provider can prevent a fetch or send attempt.

For the common case—who wrote, what did they say, and can I answer?—a short conversation from a basic phone is often enough.
