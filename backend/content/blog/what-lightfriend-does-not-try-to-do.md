Software roadmaps often treat every missing feature as a defect. Lightfriend begins with a different question: does this capability help someone stay connected while spending less attention on a device?

That is why the product is not organized around a feed, endless discovery, engagement streaks, or a catalog of things to browse. It also does not try to reproduce every control and rich-media feature from WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, and email inside SMS.

## Enough capability to leave

The useful core is narrower: let important messages reach a basic phone, collect routine updates into digests, answer deliberate questions about connected conversations, and provide a careful path to reply or send.

Constraints show up in real use. SMS has limited space. Voice can mishear names. Providers can disconnect. AI triage can make mistakes. Lightfriend’s job is to make those boundaries visible and provide practical controls, not pretend they do not exist.

## Attention is the success metric

A general assistant may optimize for how much it can do. Lightfriend is better judged by how little interaction is needed before you can return to the rest of your day.

That principle leaves room for new features, but not an obligation to copy every assistant. Ideas are welcome when they serve the same outcome; a request is not a promise that a feature will be built.
