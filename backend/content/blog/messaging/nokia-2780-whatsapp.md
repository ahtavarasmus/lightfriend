## The current answer

**The Nokia 2780 Flip no longer has a supported native WhatsApp route.** WhatsApp's own Help Center states that WhatsApp is no longer available on devices running KaiOS and that KaiOS support has ended.

Old reviews, store listings, and forum posts can be misleading because some earlier KaiOS phones did support a WhatsApp app. That historical compatibility does not mean a newly purchased or reset phone can use it now.

See the current provider notice: [Ending support for WhatsApp on KaiOS](https://faq.whatsapp.com/420008397294796).

## What Lightfriend changes

Lightfriend does not revive or sideload the discontinued KaiOS client. Instead, the Nokia sends an ordinary SMS to Lightfriend. A WhatsApp account connected separately can then supply selected text-first functions.

You can ask about recent messages, search for a person or subject, request a compact summary, receive focused alerts or digests, and initiate a supported text reply. The Nokia remains an ordinary call-and-text phone throughout.

## What remains on another device

You still need a supported device running the native WhatsApp app for account registration, initial pairing, recovery, and periodic linked-device maintenance. Keep that device updated and available. A linked session is not permanent and can require reauthorization.

## Before buying a Nokia 2780

The 2780 was sold for particular markets and carriers. Verify the exact model rather than relying on the Nokia name alone:

- LTE bands for your country and carrier;
- carrier approval and VoLTE support;
- unlocked status;
- ordinary SMS in both directions;
- group-message behavior if that matters outside Lightfriend;
- whether the device is still receiving necessary system updates.

HMD maintains a [Nokia 2780 Flip user guide](https://www.hmd.com/en_us/support/api/pdf/nokia-2780-flip-user-guide) for device-specific operation. Carrier compatibility remains a separate question.

## What you do not get

The SMS route is not native WhatsApp. The Nokia will not show WhatsApp's chat list, media gallery, stickers, statuses, video calls, or complete group-management interface. Images and voice notes do not become a faithful small-screen experience.

The final SMS leg is processed by cellular carriers and is not WhatsApp end-to-end encryption on the handset. AI summaries and urgency classification can also be wrong, so keep a direct emergency-contact route.

## Is it worth testing?

Yes, if you already want the Nokia 2780 for calls and SMS and need only selective WhatsApp awareness. If native WhatsApp is central to daily work or family coordination, an Android-based device supported by the current official app will be more predictable.
