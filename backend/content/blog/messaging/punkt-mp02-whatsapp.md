## The short answer

**The Punkt MP02 is not a native WhatsApp phone.** Punkt describes the MP02 as a minimalist 4G phone for voice, ordinary text messages, tethering, and Pigeon. Pigeon uses the Signal protocol, but it is not WhatsApp and does not make the MP02 a WhatsApp device.

That leaves three practical choices:

1. Keep WhatsApp on a separate supported smartphone and check it deliberately.
2. Leave WhatsApp and ask important contacts to use calls, SMS, or Pigeon.
3. Connect WhatsApp to Lightfriend and use selected text-first functions from the MP02 over ordinary SMS.

## How the Lightfriend route works

Lightfriend runs outside the handset. After connecting a WhatsApp account through the web dashboard, you can text Lightfriend from the MP02 to ask about recent messages, search for a sender or topic, request a summary, or initiate a supported text reply.

Focused alerts and scheduled digests can reduce the need to check manually. This is not a full WhatsApp client: the MP02 still receives ordinary SMS, and Lightfriend does not reproduce the native chat interface.

## What still requires another device

Keep a supported device with the native WhatsApp app available. WhatsApp pairing begins there, linked access can expire, and recovery or renewed authorization may be necessary later. Do not wipe or sell the only device capable of recovering the account until you have a tested backup plan.

## MP02 compatibility checklist

Before buying an MP02 for this setup, verify:

- your carrier supports the exact MP02 hardware revision and LTE bands;
- VoLTE, calling, and ordinary SMS work in your region;
- the number can exchange normal person-to-person SMS with your Lightfriend number;
- you are comfortable keeping a separate WhatsApp setup device;
- selected text access is enough, because rich media, video calls, statuses, and the full WhatsApp interface are not reproduced.

Punkt's current product page lists the phone's official features and carrier-compatibility guidance: [Punkt MP02 product information](https://www.punkt.ch/products/mp02-4g-minimalist-phone).

## What about Pigeon?

Pigeon is useful if the people you need can use a Signal-compatible communication route. It can be a cleaner solution than bridging WhatsApp because it is designed for the MP02. It does not solve the separate problem of staying reachable to contacts and groups that remain on WhatsApp.

## The honest tradeoff

The MP02 is intentionally limited. Lightfriend can preserve a narrow WhatsApp contact path, but every additional bridge adds external dependencies: WhatsApp, the linked session, Lightfriend, SMS carriers, and the MP02's carrier connection all need to work.

If your priority is complete native WhatsApp, use a supported Android or iPhone. If your priority is carrying a focused voice-and-text phone while retaining selected WhatsApp access, the MP02 plus a separate setup device and Lightfriend is a setup worth testing.
