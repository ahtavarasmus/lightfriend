## The short answer

**Any flip phone that can send ordinary text messages can use an AI assistant through Lightfriend.** The AI does not run on the phone. You text a dedicated number, Lightfriend processes the request outside the handset, and the answer comes back as SMS.

This works whether the carried phone is a Light Flip, Nokia, Punkt, Mudita, conventional feature phone, or an old smartphone configured as a minimalist device. The brand is not the integration; SMS and calls are the interface.

## What an AI assistant by SMS is good at

- answering a compact factual question;
- searching the web and returning a concise result;
- checking weather;
- finding recent messages or email from a connected account;
- summarizing a conversation or inbox slice;
- sending focused alerts or scheduled digests;
- setting supported reminders;
- invoking enabled connected tools with the controls Lightfriend provides.

The interaction has a natural stopping point. You ask for one thing, receive a short answer, and close the phone. There is no feed, recommendation carousel, or app to keep browsing.

## What it is not good at

SMS is a poor interface for maps, spreadsheets, long documents, visual design, photo-heavy conversations, video, and complex multi-step account administration. Carrier message length, delivery delays, and coverage also matter.

Lightfriend is not a way to turn a flip phone into an Android or iPhone. It preserves selected capabilities while leaving the addictive general-purpose interface behind.

## How to test it without committing to a new phone

1. Keep your current smartphone as a secured setup and recovery device.
2. Sign up for Lightfriend and confirm your [country and number route](/supported-countries).
3. Text several real requests you expect to make during a normal week.
4. Connect only the services you genuinely need.
5. Carry any spare SMS-capable phone for a reversible trial.
6. Record what still forces you back to the smartphone.

Check communication, navigation, banking, authentication, transport, payments, and recovery as separate requirements.

## Example requests

You might text:

- "What is the weather tomorrow morning?"
- "Search for the opening hours of the nearest passport office."
- "Did Alex send me anything important today?"
- "Summarize unread email from the last four hours."
- "Remind me at 17:30 to pick up the prescription."

Exact capabilities depend on the services you connect, the tools you enable, and the current product limitations. AI can misunderstand requests or produce an incorrect answer, so verify anything consequential.

## Privacy boundaries to understand

Lightfriend's production application runs inside an AWS Nitro Enclave, stored application data is encrypted, and the reported enclave measurement can be compared with published build evidence. Text inference uses an attested confidential-computing provider.

The final SMS leg is still ordinary carrier SMS, not end-to-end encrypted. Connected providers, carriers, enabled external tools, and optional voice processing have their own boundaries. Review the [privacy architecture](/privacy-architecture) and [limitations](/limitations) for the full picture.

## The deciding question

Do you need a phone that *runs AI apps*, or do you need occasional access to useful answers and connected services?

If it is the second, a simple flip phone plus an external SMS assistant can be enough—and the intentionally narrow interface is the point.
