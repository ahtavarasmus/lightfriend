# Can I Leave My Smartphone?

Yes, if the phone you choose can use ordinary SMS or calls and a supported Lightfriend number route is available to you.

Lightfriend runs outside the handset. The carried phone does not need a Lightfriend app, mobile data, a browser, an app store, or a particular operating system. SMS provides the complete text-first interface and focused alerts. Optional voice calls provide another interface for longer questions.

Interactive planner: https://lightfriend.ai/can-i-leave-my-smartphone

## The phone is not the platform

Choose a phone for its size, battery life, keyboard, appearance, durability, or how it feels to carry. Connected clients, account monitoring, AI processing, and automations run in Lightfriend's service. The handset sees an ordinary SMS conversation or call.

Light Phone II and III, Punkt MP02, Mudita Kompakt, Nokia feature phones, flip phones, candy-bar phones, and old smartphones used minimally can all provide that ordinary interface. These are examples, not a compatibility list.

## What you can keep

| Capability | Phone interface | What remains outside the phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram | SMS questions, focused alerts, digests, search, and supported replies | Provider account, pairing, recovery, rich interfaces, and periodic maintenance |
| Email | SMS search, summaries, focused alerts, digests, and supported replies | Account administration, attachments, and complex inbox work |
| AI search and weather | SMS or optional voice question and a compact answer | Search and AI processing in Lightfriend's service |
| Images and QR codes | MMS where the number route supports it | Analysis in the service; availability varies by country |
| Tesla and MCP tools | Supported text or voice requests | External authorization and third-party trust boundaries |

## What still needs checking

The handset brand does not need a Lightfriend integration, but these external constraints still matter:

- The phone must have working carrier service and an ordinary SMS or call route.
- Lightfriend number inventory, destination permissions, carrier capabilities, provider rules, and local regulation vary by country.
- WhatsApp and some other providers can require their native app on a supported device for pairing, recovery, renewed authorization, or periodic maintenance.
- Rich media, calls inside connected messaging apps, reactions, stickers, status features, and complex provider interfaces are not reproduced over SMS.
- AI classification and generated answers can be wrong, and external providers or carriers can delay or reject requests.
- The final SMS and cellular call legs are processed by carriers and are not end-to-end encrypted.

## A practical exit plan

1. Choose any phone that gives you the ordinary SMS or call experience you want.
2. List the services you cannot lose.
3. Keep a secured setup or recovery device where a connected provider requires its native app.
4. Check the current Lightfriend number route for your country.
5. Connect selected services and decide what may interrupt you.
6. Trial the workflow before moving your SIM permanently or selling your recovery device.
